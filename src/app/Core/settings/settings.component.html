Workplace Policies

Review the company's official policies and guidelines.

Attendance Policy

  • Standard working hours are 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM.
  • Employees arriving after 9:15 AM will be marked as late.
  • Three late arrivals in a month will generate a written warning.
  • Remote check-in is available for approved employees only.

Leave Policy

  • Each employee is entitled to 21 annual leave days.
  • Sick leave quota is 15 days per year with a medical certificate.
  • Leave requests must be submitted at least 3 days in advance.
  • Manager approval is required for all leave types.

Security Policy

  • Passwords must be at least 8 characters with mixed case and digits.
  • Sessions auto-expire after 30 minutes of inactivity.
  • Two-factor authentication is recommended for all admin accounts.
  • Password changes are required every 90 days.

Data Privacy

  • Employee personal data is encrypted at rest and in transit.
  • Access to HR data is restricted to authorized personnel only.
  • Data retention period is 5 years after employment ends.
  • Employees may request a copy of their data at any time.
